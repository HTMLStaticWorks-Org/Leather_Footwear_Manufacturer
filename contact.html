GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Do you support custom and private-label footwear?

Yes. We can discuss product development based on your design brief, reference sample or early concept, including materials and branding details.

Can I enquire about bulk or wholesale orders?

Absolutely. Use the enquiry form and select Wholesale / bulk order. Include the product category, estimated quantity and preferred timeline.

Can you help with samples before production?

Sampling and product review can be discussed as part of the development process. Requirements depend on the style and specifications.

What information should I share for a quotation?

Share product references, materials, size range, estimated quantities, target market and expected delivery window.

How quickly will your team respond?

We aim to respond during business hours. Response time may vary depending on the details and scope of your enquiry.
