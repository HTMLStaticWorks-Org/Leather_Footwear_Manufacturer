# Leather & Last — Footwear Manufacturers

A premium, bespoke leather footwear manufacturing and private-label production website.

🌐 **Live Website**: [https://htmlstaticworks-org.github.io/Leather_Footwear_Manufacturer/](https://htmlstaticworks-org.github.io/Leather_Footwear_Manufacturer/)

---

## Overview

Leather & Last is an editorial, responsive multi-page website tailored for luxury shoemakers, artisanal footwear brands, and wholesale manufacturing partners.

### Pages Included
- **Home 1 (`index.html`)**: Editorial sequence hero, artisanal craftsmanship highlights, curated product features, workshop statistics, and direct quote request.
- **Home 2 (`home-2.html`)**: Design studio line-up, "The Edit" interactive cards showcase, craft focus, and private-label partnership inquiries.
- **About Us (`about.html`)**: Heritage story, values behind the work, cordwainer workshop insights, and craft gallery.
- **Services (`services.html`)**: 6-step manufacturing process (Design, Material Sourcing, Lasting & Assembly, Quality Assurance, Packaging & Fulfilment, Support).
- **Products (`products.html`)**: Signature leather collections, formal derbies & oxfords, casual loafers, boots, material swatches, and interactive enquiry selection.
- **Wholesale (`wholesale.html`)**: B2B private label capabilities, retail partnership tiers, and production volume details.
- **Contact Us (`contact.html`)**: Project consultation enquiry form and interactive FAQ accordion.

---

## Features & Technical Highlights

- **Visual Curation**: 24 distinct, high-resolution artisanal footwear and workshop photography assets. Zero repeated image assets across all pages.
- **Interactive Controls**: Light / Dark theme switcher and RTL (Right-to-Left) layout toggles.
- **Responsive Architecture**: Fully optimized across desktop (1440px+), laptop (1024px), tablet (768px), and mobile (320px–480px).
- **Smooth Animations**: CSS keyframe transitions, sequence reveals, and reduced-motion accessibility support.
